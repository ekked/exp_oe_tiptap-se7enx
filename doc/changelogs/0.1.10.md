# exp_oe_tiptap 0.1.10

Not released yet. Formulas (the inline custom tags `math_latex` and `math_mathml`) are inline atoms in Tiptap: ordinary
edits around them no longer change them.

## Fixed

- Fixed: Formulas were damaged by ordinary edits in Tiptap: a second edit in the custom tag dialog (also from the
  context menu or with Alt+Shift+E) nested a copy of the tag, bold, italic or a link around a formula went inside it
  after any edit of the field (markup in the TeX source), two formulas side by side with the same attributes merged,
  Enter split a formula, text typed after it went into it and remove formatting removed it. They are now inline atoms
  (`ezCustomInlineAtom`): their text is changed in the custom tag dialog (new field Content), formatting wraps the
  whole tag, converting the paragraph to literal keeps their text. The stored XML of unedited content is unchanged;
  `s067` now comes back identical instead of "equal".
- Fixed: A line break typed into the field Content, or between two paragraphs made into a formula, was removed by
  the parser on store (`\cdot⏎x` became `\cdotx`): it becomes a space, and in TeX source (content.ini
  `[CustomTagSettings] TeXSource[]`, passed as the option `texSourceCustomTags`) a `%` comment goes with its line
  break first, the way TeX reads it. Editing a formula written as `<u>` keeps the `<u>`.
- Fixed: Editing an inline custom tag that stays formatting (a mark) in the custom tag dialog nested a second tag in
  it instead of changing its attributes.

## Added

- Added: `[EditorSettings] AtomicInlineCustomTags[]` (default `math_latex`, `math_mathml`), the inline custom tags kept
  as one piece; passed to the editor as the option `atomicInlineCustomTags`. A siteaccess adds its own.
- Added: The field Content in the Spanish (Spain and Mexico), Hebrew, Latin and Klingon translations.
- Added: `tests/js/ui/inline-custom-atoms.test.mjs` and `tests/php/InlineCustomTagRoundtripTest.php` (edits on
  formulas and on an inline custom tag through the real parser); `FixtureParityTest` expects `s067` identical.
